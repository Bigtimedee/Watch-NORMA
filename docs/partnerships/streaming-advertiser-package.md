### Subscriber Acquisition at the Moment of Maximum Engagement

*Prepared June 2026 — getnorma.app · bd@norma-app.com*

## The Opportunity

Watch NORMA's users are the most engaged live sports viewers on mobile. They open the app when the game matters — spread covering, overtime starting, buzzer approaching. These are the same users streaming services want to acquire and retain.

Advertising in NORMA's intent-moment auction means your "Watch on ESPN+" CTA appears not during a commercial break, but when a user is actively tracking a live game and your content is directly relevant to what they're doing right now.

---

## Why NORMA's Inventory Is Different

| Platform | When the ad appears | User state |
|----------|---------------------|------------|
| Social (Meta, TikTok) | Scrolling between content | Passive, low intent |
| CTV/streaming pre-roll | Before content starts | Captive but irritated |
| **NORMA** | **When a game moment triggers** | **Active, high intent, looking at game** |

NORMA's alert moments are not interruptions — they are the reason the user opened the app. A streaming ad appearing at `close_game` or `overtime` lands when the user is already engaged in the game that the streaming service carries.

---

## Available Moment Types for Streaming Campaigns

| Moment Type | Description | Recommended Streaming CTA |
|-------------|-------------|---------------------------|
| `close_game` | Margin ≤ 6 in 2nd half | "Don't miss the ending — Watch on [Service]" |
| `overtime` | Game has entered OT | "It's OT — stream it live on [Service]" |
| `game_resolved` | Final score posted | "Catch the highlights on [Service]" |
| `mlb_close_game` | Baseball tie game, 7th inning+ | "Watch on [Service]" |
| `mlb_walk_off` | Walk-off situation | "Watch the finish on [Service]" |
| `total_alert` | Game score pace tracking | "Live now on [Service]" |

Floor CPMs for streaming campaigns range from $0.20–$0.45 depending on moment type.

## Attribution

| Level | How It Works |
|-------|--------------|
| **Inferred (default)** | NORMA tracks `stream_open` events when a user taps "Watch Now" from an alert. Conversion window: 30 minutes. |
| **Verified (upgrade)** | The streaming service fires a server-to-server callback when a user completes subscription sign-up. Converts inferred attribution to verified. Available to committed partners. |

---

## No Geo-Restriction

Unlike sportsbook campaigns, streaming campaigns are not geo-restricted. Ads are eligible to serve to all NORMA users nationwide (or globally, if the streaming service has international rights). This maximizes available reach.

---

## Pricing and Access

Streaming campaigns run in NORMA's Vickrey (second-price) auction. You set a max CPM bid; you pay the second-highest bid. No minimum commitment for self-serve campaigns.

**Self-serve onboarding**: Start at `getnorma.app/onboarding?track=streaming` — streaming demand type pre-selected, moment types and bid pre-configured for subscriber acquisition.

**Brand review**: Streaming and commerce campaigns require a one-time brand safety review (typically completed within 24 hours) before entering the auction.
