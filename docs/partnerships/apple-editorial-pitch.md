# Apple Editorial Pitch — Watch NORMA

## Two-Paragraph Editorial Pitch

Watch NORMA is a real-time sports intelligence app that tells fans and bettors exactly when a game is worth watching based on what they personally have at stake. Rather than flooding users with generic score notifications, NORMA evaluates live play-by-play data, odds movement, and the user's own wagers to fire a single, precisely timed alert: your spread is live with four minutes left, here is why this moment matters. The app uses Sign In with Apple, native push notifications through APNs, and a multi-step deep link system covering 24 partner apps including ESPN, DraftKings, FanDuel, and the NBA, so users can go from an alert to watching the game in one tap.

NORMA was built for the iPhone from the ground up using React Native's New Architecture (Bridgeless / JSI), automatic dark mode, and bet slip scanning via the camera that uses computer vision to extract wager details instantly. The alert engine respects quiet hours, caps notifications per game, and includes a structured "Why Now" explanation with every alert so users always know the context and not just the score. With March Madness, the NBA Playoffs, and MLB all converging in spring, NORMA is positioned as the essential companion for the tens of millions of Americans following live sports with real money on the line.
